\subsection*{Leaderboard and efficiency}
We evaluated 34 configurations of 21 models under four harnesses (Pi, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Grok Build), each making 3 attempts per evaluation without internet access, for 9,282 runs. After excluding platform failures (66) and refusals (4), 9,212 graded attempts remained.

No configuration passed more than 33\% of attempts (Figure~\ref{fig:leaderboard}A). Claude Opus 5.5 with the Pi harness ranked first at 32.2\% (95\% CI \mbox{23.5--41.0}), followed by Opus 5 / Pi (30.8\%) and Sonnet 5.5 / Claude Code (29.7\%). The top five configurations were all Claude models, and the confidence intervals of the top ten overlapped. Grok 4.7 / Grok Build (27.5\%, sixth) was the strongest non-Anthropic configuration, and Nemotron 3.5 Lightning / Pi was the weakest (7.7\%). The mean pass rate across all 34 configurations was 21.0\%. The low ceiling does not reflect unsolvable tasks: 78 of the 91 evaluations were solved by at least one configuration, and 61 were solved on every attempt by at least one. The 13 evaluations that no configuration solved come from 10 problems, and include both pseudoreplication evaluations built on a glaucoma retina dataset and recording-level spike-sorter accuracy from SpikeForest.

Across the 29 configurations with reported cost, accuracy rose with spend (Spearman $\rho$ = 0.64), but the relationship was loose (\mbox{Figure~\ref{fig:leaderboard}B--E}). GPT-6 Luna / Pi passed 20.4\% of attempts at about \$0.03 per run, within about 12 points of the leader at roughly one-hundredth of its cost, while the most expensive configuration, Fable 5.1 / Claude Code (\$5.22 per run), passed 23.8\%. Cost was not reported for five of the six Codex configurations, which are omitted from this comparison.

 Across all configuration--evaluation pairs, 68.1\% were never solved in three attempts, 11.4\% were solved every time, and the remaining 20.5\% were solved on some attempts but not others (Figure~\ref{fig:leaderboard}F). Even the leading configuration solved 42.9\% of evaluations at least once, but only 23.1\% on all three attempts.

The harness mattered for some models but did not favor any single design (Figure~\ref{fig:leaderboard}G). Opus 5 gained 5.1 points under Pi relative to Claude Code and Fable 5.1 gained 4.6 points, whereas Sonnet 5.5 did better under Claude Code (by 2.6 points), and Grok 4.7 did better under Grok Build than under Pi (by 4.0 points). For the six OpenAI models, Codex and Pi were within 2.6 points of each other in every case.
